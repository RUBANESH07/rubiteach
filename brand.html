Brand guide · v1.0

Rubitech Academy logo

The RT monogram joins the R and T with one shared top bar, a single continuous line that reads as a line of code. The coral leg is the only accent, the spark that sets the mark apart.

Primary mark

Use the badge wherever space is tight: app icon, favicon, avatars, social.

Lockups

Horizontal lockup for headers and documents. Use the reverse version on plum or other dark backgrounds.

One colour

For stamps, print and single-ink uses. The mark takes the colour of its container.

Sizes & clear space

Keep clear space equal to the width of the T stem (x) on every side. Minimum size: 16 px for the badge, 120 px wide for the lockup.

Colour

Warm plum with a coral spark on a blush canvas.

Do and don't

  • ✅ Use the supplied SVG files; never redraw the letters.
  • ✅ Keep the leg coral on plum and cream badges. In one-colour use the leg takes the same colour.
  • ❌ Don't stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows to the mark.
  • ❌ Don't recolour the badge outside the palette above.
  • ❌ Don't set coral text on cream at small sizes; it fails contrast. Use plum.
